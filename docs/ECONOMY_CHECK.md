# Economy check (Phase 1)

Generated by `pnpm economy-check` (packages/scoring/src/replay). Do not edit the numbers by hand; re-run instead.

- Scoring rules: `config/scoring.v0.3.json` (substitute also replaces a starter who played under 30 minutes and landed nothing; captaincy passes to the sub: yes), basic feed only: yes.
- Economy: `config/economy.v0.1.json` (version 0.1).
- Data: public 2025/26 FPL dataset snapshot (`data/fpl-2025-26`), gameweeks 4–38 (35 simulated; the first three have no "previous four gameweeks" history, as in `tools/sim/sim.py`).
- 150 seeded seasons per scenario. Every gameweek is scored by the production engine (`scoreSquad`), so captain, bold action, rarity multipliers, the sub rule, double and blank gameweeks are all included. Re-running gives identical totals (covered by `test/replay.test.ts`).

## What the strategies do

- **Casual** = taps Auto-pick every week: highest-average Likely/Possible action ×2 per player, auto captain, no bold action. **Casual + bold** also marks their most likely action as bold.
- **Smart** = picks the two highest-average actions from any tier (no chaos), captains the best expected scorer, and uses the bold action only when it has positive expected value.
- **Always goal**, **chaos chaser** and **random** are the comparison strategies from the Python sim (chaos chaser also always bolds its highest-value action).
- **Persistent squad** = one random six-card squad drawn from the regular-starter pool at GW4 and kept all season (injuries, transfers and blanks hit it, so the sub rule matters). **Redrawn** = a fresh squad of regular starters each week (the Python sim's method; an upper bound for an active player who keeps their five fresh).

Findings:

1. **Average points per gameweek:** casual 18.8 (persistent) to 22.0 (redrawn); smart 21.8 to 25.1. A full 38-week season is roughly 715–954 Season Points.
2. **Skill pays, but Auto-pick is strong.** Smart beats casual by 16% (persistent) and 14% (redrawn), and beats "always goal" by 26%. The strategies still do not read fixtures, so the real skill edge should be larger. Because Auto-pick is close to optimal on average, consider making it pick from Likely only (or randomise among the top three) if testers find it removes the decision.
3. **Captain and bold add about 1.4 points/GW for a casual player** who bolds their safest action. Smart players' bold choice is EV-positive only on high-hit-rate actions, so bold is a small, safe edge rather than a swing; the bold penalty mostly bites players who bold long shots.
4. **Chaos chasing loses** (-11.0 pts/GW, negative in 72% of weeks), so chaos picks and big bold punts cannot win a league. Season Points can go negative for them; Coins are floored at 0 per week in this model (the ledger never debits for a bad week).
5. **Rarity gap:** with the rarity multipliers, an all-Elite squad scores 1.17× an all-Bronze one (spec target ≈1.10×). Still well below FPL's raw quality gap, but wider than the design note. If the closed test confirms it, widen the multipliers (e.g. Bronze 1.25 / Silver 1.12 / Gold 1.0 / Elite 0.85) and re-run.

Is 100 / 60 / 400 "roughly one new card per week including the weekly free pack"?

- **Yes.** A casual player earns 18.8 Coins/GW: a player pack every 5.3 weeks, a swap every 3.2 weeks, a squad pack about every 21 weeks. With the free pack that is 1.19 cards/GW (smart: 1.22).
- The free pack is 84% of a casual player's new cards, so **confirming actions each week is the main card source** and Coins are a top-up. That is the intended retention lever; it also means a player who misses weeks (carried-over actions do not unlock the pack) falls behind on collection but not on the leaderboard.
- The squad pack is the cheapest card per Coin (80 vs 100); fine as a "save up" target, roughly twice a season.

**Recommendation on prices: keep 100 / 60 / 400 for the closed test.** No change needed.

**Milestones need adjusting.** With 250 / 500 / 750 / 1000, the average casual player reaches 2 of 4 milestones in a season and the top one (1000) only an above-average smart player with a strong squad reaches. Proposed: **200 / 400 / 600 / 800** (top milestone ≈ 110% of a casual 38-week season), so a regular casual player sees three or four rewards spread across the season.

## Caveats

- Hit rates in the config come from the same 2025/26 data, so "smart" has hindsight about average rates (but not about individual matches).
- Basic feed only; advanced-feed actions (tackles, CBI, defensive contribution) are excluded, which is why totals differ from the Python baseline (`tools/sim/baseline/summary.json`, which included them and had no bold action or rarity multipliers).
- Squads ignore the two-per-club limit and the squad rating cap; card upgrades over the season are not modelled.
